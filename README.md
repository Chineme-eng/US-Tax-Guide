# 🇺🇸 US Tax Guide – Compare State Taxes & Calculate Take-Home Pay

A free, open-source **U.S. tax calculator and comparison tool** that helps you estimate your **take-home income by state**, including federal, state, and optional local taxes.

---

## 🔍 What Is This?

The **US Tax Guide** is an interactive web tool that lets you:

* Compare **state income tax rates**
* Estimate **after-tax income (take-home pay)**
* Analyze **sales tax and property tax differences**
* Explore which states let you **keep more of your money**

👉 Perfect for:

* People considering moving states
* Freelancers & remote workers
* Anyone optimizing income and cost of living

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 💰 Take-Home Pay Calculator

Estimate your net income after:

* Federal income tax (IRS brackets)
* FICA (Social Security + Medicare)
* State income tax
* Optional **local/city income taxes** (NYC, etc.)

---

### 📊 State Tax Comparison Tool

Compare all 50 states across:

* Income tax
* Sales tax (state + local averages)
* Property tax

---

### 🏙 Local Tax Support

Includes optional local taxes for:

* New York City
* Yonkers
* (Expandable system for more cities)

---

### ⚡ Fast, No Backend

* Runs entirely in your browser
* No login, no API, no database
* Built for speed and simplicity

## 📦 How to Use

1. Enter your income
2. Select a state
3. (Optional) choose a local tax (if applicable)
4. View your estimated take-home pay
5. Compare with another state

---

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This tool provides **estimates only** and is for educational purposes.

It does NOT include:

* All deductions or credits
* Complex filing scenarios
* Exact IRS/state calculations

Always consult a professional for official tax advice.
